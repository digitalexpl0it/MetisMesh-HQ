# Safe drinking water

Community reference only — not a substitute for local public-health guidance.

## Priorities

1. **Known-safe sealed water** (commercial bottles, previously treated storage).
2. **Boil** clear water at a rolling boil for at least **1 minute** (3 minutes above ~2,000 m / 6,500 ft).
3. **Filter** cloudy water first (cloth, coffee filter, or a household filter), then boil or disinfect.
4. **Chemical disinfection** only when boiling is impossible — follow product labels (bleach or iodine kits).

## Storage

- Use clean, food-grade containers with tight lids.
- Keep out of sunlight and away from fuels or chemicals.
- Label the date treated; rotate stored water on a schedule your site agrees on.

## Do not

- Do not drink flood water, radiator fluid, or untreated surface water without a plan.
- Do not mix cleaning chemicals to “make” disinfectant.

If someone is severely dehydrated or ill after drinking questionable water, treat it as a medical emergency and use your local help channels.
