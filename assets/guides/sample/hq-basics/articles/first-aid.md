# Basic first aid priorities

Not medical advice. Get trained help when you can.

## Scene and self

1. Make sure **you** are safe before approaching.
2. Call or radio for help if the person is unresponsive, bleeding heavily, or struggling to breathe.
3. Wear gloves if available; wash hands after.

## Heavy bleeding

- Apply **firm direct pressure** with a clean cloth.
- Do not remove soaked cloths — add more on top.
- Elevate the limb if it does not worsen the injury and you know how.
- Use a tourniquet only if you are trained and bleeding is life-threatening on a limb.

## Unresponsive person

- Check responsiveness and breathing.
- If trained, begin **CPR** and use an AED if one is available.
- Keep the airway clear; place in the recovery position only if they are breathing and you are trained to do so.

## Burns and breaks

- Cool burns with clean cool water (not ice) for several minutes; cover loosely.
- Immobilize suspected fractures; do not straighten limbs.

Write down time, what happened, and what you did — that helps the next caregiver on the mesh or in person.
