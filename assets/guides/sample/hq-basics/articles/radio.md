# Mesh radio etiquette

Keep the mesh usable for the whole community during an outage.

## Keep it short

- Mesh text is limited (~160 bytes). Prefer facts over essays.
- One idea per message. Use channel names your HQ agreed on.

## Be clear

- Lead with **who / where / what / need** when asking for help.
- Avoid slang that outsiders on the channel will not understand.
- Do not send channel secrets or PINs in public channels.

## Be kind to the airtime

- Wait before re-sending; duplicates clog the mesh.
- Prefer HQ bot commands (`!weather`, and similar) only when enrolled and needed.
- Leave emergency traffic room — yield if someone is coordinating aid.

## HQ operators

- Enrollment and private channel secrets stay on this headquarters.
- Guides in this app are local reference only; they are not sent over the radio unless an operator copies text by hand.
