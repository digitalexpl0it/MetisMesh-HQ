# Chemical emergency — shelter in place

Community reference only — **not** a gas-mask training manual and **not** medical advice. Follow local officials and the manufacturer manual for any respirator or issued gear you actually own.

## If officials say shelter in place

1. Go indoors immediately. Close windows, doors, and fireplace dampers.
2. Turn off fans, HVAC, and anything that pulls outside air in.
3. Move to an interior room with as few windows as practical.
4. Seal gaps with plastic sheeting and tape **only if** that is what local guidance asks for and you have the materials ready.
5. Listen on battery/hand-crank radio or official channels for “all clear.”
6. When told it is safe, air out the building as directed.

## Issued respirators / masks

- Use **only** equipment your organization issued and trained you on.
- Fit, filters, and change-out schedules come from the **device manual** and your safety officer — not from mesh chat.
- A cloth face covering is **not** a chemical respirator.

## Do not

- Do not invent filters from household materials for toxic atmospheres.
- Do not “test” sealed rooms with real chemicals.
- Do not ignore evacuation orders because you think a room seal is enough.

## Afterward

- Seek medical care for breathing trouble, burning eyes, or unexplained illness.
- Tell responders what you were exposed to if you know.
