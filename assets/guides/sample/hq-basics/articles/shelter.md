# Shelter: stay or go

Community reference only — follow local officials when they give direction. This is not an evacuation order.

## Decide early

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|
| Local officials say evacuate | Leave on the advised route; take go-bag, meds, IDs, radio |
| Storm / wind without flood risk | Interior room, lowest floor that stays dry, away from windows |
| Flash flood / rising water | Higher ground or higher floor; never a sealed attic |
| Extreme heat | Coolest interior space; shade; check on neighbors if safe |
| Extreme cold / outage | One warm room; seal drafts; never heat with charcoal indoors |

## Stay-in-place checklist

- Know which room is safest for wind vs flood vs chemical advice from officials.
- Keep several days of water, food, meds, light, and a battery/hand-crank radio.
- Charge phones and radios while power is up; conserve afterward.
- Text instead of calling when the network is congested.

## Go-bag (grab and leave)

- Water, snacks, meds, copies of IDs, cash if useful locally
- Flashlight, spare batteries, whistle
- Weather-appropriate layers; sturdy shoes
- Mesh / radio charged if that is how your group coordinates

## Do not

- Do not drive or walk through flood water (“turn around, don’t drown”).
- Do not run a generator, grill, or camp stove indoors or in a garage — carbon monoxide kills.
- Do not wait for perfect information if officials have already ordered evacuation.

After an event, return only when local officials say it is safe.
